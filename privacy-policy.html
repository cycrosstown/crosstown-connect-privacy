In short: Crosstown Connect is an internal coordination app for Crosstown Younglife leaders and volunteers — used to share event details, prayer requests, and messages with each other. It is not intended for use by students or anyone under 18. We only collect what's needed to run those features, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account or content at any time.

Section 1

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Crosstown Younglife ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information through the Crosstown Connect mobile app (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Crosstown Connect exists to serve as a hub for Crosstown Younglife leaders: staying in the know on events, coordinating logistics, sharing prayer requests, and communicating with fellow leaders.

Section 2

Who can use this app

Crosstown Connect is built for adult Crosstown Younglife leaders and volunteers. It is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by minors, including the students served by our ministry. Access is limited to individuals we invite as leaders or staff.

Section 3

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the App's features:

Account information

Your name and email address, used to create your login and identify you to other leaders in the App.

Messages & leader communications

Content you send through the App's messaging features, so it can be delivered to and stored for other leaders in the conversation.

Prayer requests

The content of prayer requests you choose to submit. We treat this as sensitive information — see Retention & Deletion for how it's handled.

Event & coordination content

Event details, RSVPs, or coordination notes you or other leaders enter into the App.

Push notification token

A device identifier issued by Apple that lets us deliver notifications (like new prayer requests or event updates) to your device. This token isn't tied to your identity beyond your own account.

Basic technical data

Standard diagnostic information (like app version and crash logs) automatically generated by our app platform to keep the App running reliably.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity outside the App.

Section 4

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your leader account
  • To deliver messages, prayer requests, and event information between leaders
  • To send push notifications you'd expect from the App (new messages, prayer requests, event changes)
  • To keep the App secure and working properly

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles from it.

Section 5

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. It's shared only with the service providers that make the App work, and with other leaders inside the App itself:

WhoWhy
Other Crosstown Younglife leadersMessages, prayer requests, and event content are visible to other leaders in the App by design.
Base44Our app development and backend hosting platform. Base44 stores App data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.
Apple, Inc.Delivers push notifications and handles App Store distribution.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our leaders and community.

Section 6

Retention & deletion

  • Leaders can delete individual messages or content they've posted directly within the App at any time.
  • Staff administrators can remove unwanted or outdated content, including prayer requests, as part of routine upkeep.
  • If you'd like your account and associated data fully deleted, email cycrosstown@gmail.com and we'll process the request within a reasonable time.
  • We aim to keep data only as long as it's useful for its purpose — we periodically clear out old or resolved prayer requests rather than keeping them indefinitely.

Section 7

Children's privacy

Crosstown Connect is intended solely for adult leaders and volunteers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will delete the associated information promptly. If you believe a minor has accessed the App, please contact us at cycrosstown@gmail.com.

Section 8

Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, including those provided by our platform Base44, to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep leader and ministry information protected.

Section 9

Your choices & rights

  • Access or correct your account information by contacting us or updating it in the App.
  • Delete content you've posted, or request full account deletion.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time through your device's iOS Settings.

To exercise any of these, email cycrosstown@gmail.com.

Section 10

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and notify leaders through the App or by email.
