In short: Crosstown Connect is a coordination hub for Crosstown Younglife — used by staff/leaders to share event details, prayer requests, and group messages with volunteers, parents, and students. Staff and leaders can post; parents and students have view-only access to events and group messages, with no prayer request visibility and no private one-on-one messaging. The App requires users to be at least 13, and student accounts require a linked parent or guardian. We only collect what's needed to run these features, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete your account or content at any time.

Section 1

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Crosstown Younglife ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information through the Crosstown Connect mobile app (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you (or, for a student under 18, your parent or guardian) agree to the practices described here.

Crosstown Connect exists to serve as a hub for Crosstown Younglife: staff and leaders share event details, prayer requests, and coordination messages, while parents and students can stay in the know on upcoming events and group announcements.

Section 2

Who can use this app, and what each role can do

Crosstown Connect has three types of accounts, each with different access:

RoleCan do
Staff / AdminPost and manage group messages, events, and prayer requests. Adult leaders and volunteers only.
ParentView upcoming events and group messages. Cannot post content, cannot see prayer requests, cannot send or receive private one-on-one messages.
StudentView upcoming events and group messages. Cannot post content, cannot see prayer requests, cannot send or receive private one-on-one messages with anyone in the App.

Minimum age: The App is not intended for anyone under 13. Every account requires a date of birth at signup, and accounts for users under 13 will not be created or will be removed if discovered. Because a student account belongs to a minor, it must be linked to a parent or guardian's email, and new student accounts may require staff approval before becoming active.

There is no feature in the App that allows a student to privately message another student, a parent, or a staff member. All messaging visible to students and parents is group-level content posted by staff.

Section 3

Information we collect

What we collect depends on your role in the App:

Account information (all roles)

Name, email address, date of birth (used only to confirm the 13+ minimum age), and role (staff, parent, or student).

Parent/guardian linkage (student accounts)

A student account is linked to a parent or guardian's name and email, so parents are aware of and connected to their student's account.

Group messages & announcements

Content posted by staff/leaders, viewable by parents and students. Parents and students do not generate message content themselves.

Prayer requests (staff/leaders only)

The content of prayer requests submitted by staff and leaders. This is not visible to parent or student accounts. We treat this as sensitive information — see Retention & Deletion for how it's handled.

Event & coordination content

Event details posted by staff, viewable by all roles.

Push notification token

A device identifier issued by Apple that lets us deliver notifications (like new events or group announcements) to your device. This token isn't tied to your identity beyond your own account.

Basic technical data

Standard diagnostic information (like app version and crash logs) automatically generated by our app platform to keep the App running reliably.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity outside the App, and we do not use any account information — including student accounts — for advertising or behavioral tracking of any kind.

Section 4

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your leader account
  • To deliver messages, prayer requests, and event information between leaders
  • To send push notifications you'd expect from the App (new messages, prayer requests, event changes)
  • To keep the App secure and working properly

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles from it.

Section 5

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. It's shared only with the service providers that make the App work, and with other leaders inside the App itself:

WhoWhy
Other staff/leadersMessages, prayer requests, and event content are visible to other staff and leaders in the App by design.
Parents & studentsSee only group messages and events posted by staff — never prayer requests, and never each other's private information.
Base44Our app development and backend hosting platform. Base44 stores App data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.
Apple, Inc.Delivers push notifications and handles App Store distribution.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our leaders and community.

Section 6

Retention & deletion

  • Staff/admins can delete individual messages, events, or prayer requests they've posted directly within the App at any time.
  • Staff administrators can remove unwanted or outdated content, including prayer requests, as part of routine upkeep.
  • If you'd like an account — including a student account — and its associated data fully deleted, email cycrosstown@gmail.com and we'll process the request within a reasonable time. Parents may request deletion of their student's account on the student's behalf.
  • We aim to keep data only as long as it's useful for its purpose — we periodically clear out old or resolved prayer requests rather than keeping them indefinitely.

Section 7

Children's privacy

Crosstown Connect allows students aged 13 and older to use a limited, view-only version of the App as part of Crosstown Younglife's ministry programs. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 13 to create an account, and every account requires a date of birth at signup to confirm eligibility. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will delete the associated information promptly.

Every student account is linked to a parent or guardian's email at signup, so parents are aware of and connected to their student's use of the App. Students cannot post content, cannot view prayer requests, and have no way to privately message anyone — staff, parents, or other students — through the App.

Parents may contact us at any time at cycrosstown@gmail.com to review, question, or request deletion of their student's account information.

Section 8

Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, including those provided by our platform Base44, to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep leader and ministry information protected.

Section 9

Your choices & rights

  • Access or correct your account information by contacting us or updating it in the App.
  • Delete content you've posted, or request full account deletion.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time through your device's iOS Settings.

To exercise any of these, email cycrosstown@gmail.com.

Section 10

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and notify leaders through the App or by email.
