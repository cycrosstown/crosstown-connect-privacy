About

What is Crosstown Connect?

Crosstown Connect is the app for our Crosstown Younglife community. Staff and leaders use it to share upcoming events, group announcements, and coordination details. Parents and students can sign in to stay in the know on what's happening.

Access is limited to people connected to our ministry. If you're trying to join and don't have an invite, reach out at the email above.

Common questions

Help with the app

I can't sign in.

First, double-check the email address you're using matches the one your invite was sent to. If you're still stuck, email us and we can look up your account or resend your invite.

My student needs an account.

Student accounts require a parent or guardian's email on file and are approved by our staff. Email us with your student's name and your contact info and we'll get them set up.

Why can't my student post or message anyone?

That's by design. Students and parents have view-only access to events and group announcements. There is no private one-on-one messaging in the app for anyone — all content students see is posted by staff to the whole group.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are turned on for Crosstown Connect in your device's Settings app, under Notifications. If they're already on and you're still not receiving them, let us know.

I want to delete my account.

Email us and we'll delete your account and its associated information. Parents can request deletion of their student's account on their behalf.

I saw something concerning in the app.

Please tell us right away at the email above, or use the "Report a concern" option in the app. We take this seriously and will follow up.
